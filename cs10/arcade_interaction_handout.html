Arcade sprite interaction templates

Choose your interaction and get your sprite working

1

Keyboard movement

Sprite moves with arrow keys or WASD. Good for platformers, top-down games, runners.

↑ ↓ ← → or WASD
2

Mouse movement

Sprite follows your mouse cursor. Good for physics games, simulations, interactive scenes.

cursor → sprite follows
3

Click detection

Sprite responds when you click on it (or in general). Good for puzzle games, narratives, simulations.

click → sprite reacts
4

Bouncing

Sprite moves back and forth on its own. Good for animations, testing physics, passive movement.

← → ← → (repeat)

Your task

  1. Each group member picks a different template from the 4 above.
  2. Use your paper prototype image — crop it to a single sprite and save it.
  3. Write code (with AI help) that loads your sprite and makes it respond to your chosen interaction.
  4. Test it — does your sprite do what it should?
  5. Show it off — we'll run everyone's version and see them all in action.

Quick tips

Image format: Save your sprite as a PNG or JPG file in your project folder.

AI assistance: Tell Claude your template choice, show it your sprite image, and ask it to generate the Arcade code for you.

File paths: Make sure your image path is correct in your code (e.g., images/my_sprite.png).
